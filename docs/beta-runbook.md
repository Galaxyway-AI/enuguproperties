# Controlled beta runbook

## Before a beta seller starts

Confirm `BETA_MODE=true`, staging noindex headers, Paystack test mode, a legally approved test agreement, staff MFA, working email/Turnstile/R2, recent maintenance success and no aged quarantine backlog. Mark the account as a beta participant and state whether its records are test or genuine beta data.

## Registration and submission

Watch confirmation-email delivery and failed-auth metrics. Confirm profile and seller relationship, accurate property category, private evidence in quarantine and public photographs in the media bucket. Never copy documents into chat or support tickets. A submission must remain non-public until moderation.

## Moderation

Compare description, location area, price, media and declared relationship. Record discrepancies and request changes through the workflow. Advertising plan never changes the evidence standard. Confirm quarantined evidence cannot support a completed verification.

## Inspection and verification

Assign an active named inspector. Follow the site-inspection SOP. A second authorised staff member reviews evidence before a public status is awarded. Use only active verification types and record scope, limitation, date and supporting clean evidence.

## Buyer enquiries

Keep the introduction in the platform, acknowledge it, assign a responsible person and avoid exposing seller contact or private coordinates. Diaspora enquiries route through `diaspora@enuguproperties.com`.

## Problem escalation

Pause a listing for identity, authority, safety, fraud, privacy or payment concerns. Preserve audit data, restrict access and escalate to the designated operational owner. Do not make legal or title conclusions outside the approved process.

## End of beta

Export defects and feedback, classify each by security, workflow, usability or operations, close critical/high defects, remove QA data, reconcile orders and document evidence, and decide whether each real beta listing remains published.
