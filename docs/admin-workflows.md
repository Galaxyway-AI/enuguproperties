# Staff workflows

Register actual staff accounts, assign roles through an authorised database process and verify TOTP. The navigation reflects permissions; the database repeats all authorisation checks. Typical flow:

1. Open the moderation queue and select a submission. Review description, seller record, submitted media, location, risk flags and available evidence.
2. Start review. Request changes with clear reasons, reject with a reason, or approve. Moderators cannot approve their own listing. Approval establishes the publication and expiry dates.
3. Assign an inspection to an actual inspector user ID. Inspector access is restricted to assigned records. Upload dated inspection evidence, then complete the observations record. A verification officer separately reviews it before a site badge is possible.
4. Record each verification type with the relevant private evidence, clear public scope, dates and professional attribution where required.
5. Qualify enquiries and respond to offers. Create a transaction only from a recorded introduction. Progress milestones in order. For new sales support, propose the exact 2%–10% rate and service terms, obtain the seller's separate acceptance before services begin, and confirm that the completed sale qualifies. On completion, calculate only that agreed fee. Historical mandates require evidence of a separately signed agreement before their original rate can be used. Finance records invoicing/payment reconciliation.
6. Investigate property reports, record support resolutions and suspend/restrict accounts through compliance where necessary. Suspension removes live seller inventory from public access.

Editorial content, location hierarchy and advertising plans are editable through permission-controlled settings. There is no default sales commission on advert submission. Initial staff/provider selection uses internal IDs; a searchable people picker is a usability improvement still to complete. Professional provider credentials and legally reviewed agreement publication require an authorised operational process; no fake credentials or ready-to-accept legal wording is seeded.
