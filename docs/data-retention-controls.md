# Data retention controls

Enugu Properties keeps audit, agreement, order, payment and transaction records according to the approved legal retention schedule. Until that schedule is approved, operators must preserve these records and restrict access rather than delete them.

Temporary failed or abandoned video uploads are removed by the maintenance job after two hours. Rejected documents remain private and access-controlled until the approved retention period permits secure deletion. Public listing media can be removed when a listing is withdrawn, provided evidence and audit records remain intact.

Account closure must disable access, unpublish active inventory, preserve immutable financial and agreement history, and create an audit entry. A deletion request must be logged, identity-checked and reviewed by an authorised privacy operator. Export or deletion tooling must be tested on a non-production branch before it is enabled for beta customers.
