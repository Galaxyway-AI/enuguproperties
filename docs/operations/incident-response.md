# Incident response SOP

1. Record the detection time, affected environment, reporter and observable symptoms without copying secrets or customer documents.
2. Contain the incident: pause affected feature flags, restrict accounts or listings, rotate compromised credentials and preserve logs and audit history.
3. Classify impact across authentication, personal data, payments, listing integrity, availability and third parties. Escalate immediately to the operational owner for suspected fraud, data exposure or payment compromise.
4. Eradicate the cause, review adjacent paths and prepare a reversible fix. Validate it in staging with representative test accounts.
5. Recover in small steps. Confirm health, maintenance, email, storage, payment and audit signals before restoring traffic or a feature.
6. Record the timeline, decisions, affected records, notifications, evidence and corrective actions. Legal or regulatory notifications require authorised professional review.
7. Complete a retrospective and assign owners and dates for every corrective action before closing the incident.
