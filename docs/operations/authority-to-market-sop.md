# Authority to market SOP

For an owner, record the ownership relationship and available supporting evidence. For an agent, require a written mandate or independently recorded owner confirmation. For a company, record the organisation and authority of the representative. Joint, family, estate and disputed property require escalation and must not receive a completed status from ordinary moderation. Check names, property reference, scope, dates and signatories; record discrepancies. This review concerns marketing authority and does not establish legal title or authority to complete a transfer.
