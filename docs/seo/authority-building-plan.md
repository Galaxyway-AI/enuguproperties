# Authority-building plan

Enugu Properties should earn references by being useful to Enugu buyers, sellers and professionals. Bulk directory submissions, paid link packages, private blog networks and fabricated statistics are excluded.

## First 90 days

1. Complete official brand profiles with consistent name, production URL, support contact and truthful Lagos registered address. Do not claim an Enugu office.
2. Invite genuine Enugu developers and agencies to maintain accurate inventory and link to their live property pages where appropriate.
3. Build relationships with qualified solicitors, surveyors and estate surveyors for reviewed educational contributions. Identify credentials only with permission.
4. Offer local media a factual story about the marketplace’s launch, moderation model, diaspora enquiry process and available inventory.
5. Promote the five initial guides through owned social channels and answer real user questions with links to the relevant guide.

## Link-worthy assets after data matures

- quarterly Enugu inventory report;
- area distribution of active listings;
- median asking-price ranges with clear sample sizes;
- days-listed and sold/withdrawn observations where status data is reliable;
- diaspora buyer question survey;
- anonymised property safety and fraud-report themes.

Every data release must state that it uses Enugu Properties marketplace adverts, distinguish asking prices from valuations and enforce a minimum sample size. Confidential or user-level data must never be exposed.

## Potential relationship categories

Enugu business groups, developers, reputable agencies, NIESV-linked professionals, survey and legal professionals, universities and researchers, local newsrooms, diaspora community organisations and relevant Nigerian business directories.

Owner approval is required before press outreach, paid sponsorship, partnership commitments, account creation or signing directory/association terms.
